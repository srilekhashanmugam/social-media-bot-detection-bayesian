02 — BAYESIAN INFERENCE

How the detection works

01

Prior Probability

The model starts with an assumed baseline probability that an account is a bot.

02

Evidence

Account age, posting frequency, follower behaviour and posting patterns are treated as evidence.

03

Posterior Probability

Bayesian updating combines the prior and observed evidence to estimate the final bot probability.

BAYES' THEOREM

P(Bot | Evidence)

The prototype estimates how likely an account is to be a bot after considering the available behavioural evidence.

Prototype Notice

This project is an educational Bayesian inference prototype. It does not access or monitor real social media accounts and should not be treated as a definitive bot detection system.
